# ParkAlert India Pilot Runbook

## Sticker placement
- Place the QR sticker on the rear windshield or directly above/below the rear number plate for maximum visibility.
- Keep the QR clear of obstructions (tinted film, dirt) and align it so that security staff can scan from the rear of the vehicle.
- Use a 6 cm × 6 cm sticker with a matte finish and a laminate overlay to withstand rain and dust.

## Pilot rollout recommendations
- Start with one secure apartment complex or a gated parking area.
- Target ~50 vehicles with consenting owners and assign one security team to monitor the flow.
- Provide brief training (10–15 minutes) to the security team on how to scan, select a reason, and add notes before sending alerts.
- Encourage owners to keep the app installed with notifications enabled and quiet hours configured as needed.

## Feedback form (collect after each pilot day)
- **Time to respond** (e.g., within X minutes from alert to resolution).
- **False/spam alerts count** (how many alerts had no valid reason).
- **Usability rating** (1–5 for ease of use scanning + resolving).
- **Language needs** (additional languages or translations requested).
